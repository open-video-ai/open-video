## Timing & sequencing advice

### When to post (the whole-thread answer in one paragraph)
Post the thread at **06:15 PT on a Tuesday or Wednesday** (per `LAUNCH_PLAN.md §1`). Rationale:
Tue/Wed outperform Fri–Mon on X engagement; 06:15 PT catches the US East-Coast morning scroll
(09:15 ET) AND the European afternoon (14:15 GMT), which are the two biggest English-language
engagement windows in a single post. Avoid US holidays, Fridays, and days with major
Apple/NVIDIA/OpenAI keynotes — those drown every other announcement. Pin the thread the second it's
live, and quote-tweet Tweet 1 from the founder's personal account and any advisor accounts.

### Per-tweet pacing
- **Tweet 1 first, alone.** Let it sit ~2–3 minutes so the clip indexes and the thread-root is
  established. Pin immediately.
- **Then post Tweets 2–11 as a connected thread** (X's "add another tweet" composer), or use a
  scheduler (Typefully/Hypefury/Buffer) with a 30–60s gap between each. Do NOT dump all 11 at once
  with zero gap — X throttles rapid-fire threads from new-ish accounts and the algorithm likes to
  see early engagement on the root before children appear.
- **The whole thread should be live within ~10 minutes of 06:15 PT.** Then stop posting and start
  replying.

### The first 4 hours decide the thread
Be **live and replying for the first 4 hours**. Answer every reply in <60 minutes. The algorithm
weighs reply-rate heavily in the first hour; a thread that gets 20 substantive replies in hour one
gets shown to 10× more timelines than one that gets them in hour four. Pre-canned answers ready
(see Reply strategy below) so you're pasting + personalizing, not composing under pressure.

### Amplification moves, in order of ROI
1. **Founder personal account** quote-tweets Tweet 1 with one line of context ("we just open-sourced
   this — link in thread"). Highest-trust amplification you have.
2. **DM the thread to 10–15 friendly accounts** in the ComfyUI / open-weights / AI-video orbit
   *before* posting, with a heads-up to RT if they like it. Warm RTs from named accounts in the
   first 30 min beat any paid push.
3. **Cross-post the hook to Reddit r/StableDiffusion / r/LocalLLaMA / r/comfyui** per
   `LAUNCH_PLAN.md §7` — but stagger (07:30→09:00 PT) so each sub gets its own comment window and
   you're not splitting attention.
4. **Show HN** at 09:30 PT, linking the repo (not the thread) as the canonical.
5. **Product Hunt** at 00:01 PT (already live) — every downstream post drives votes upward into an
   already-live listing.

### What NOT to do
- Don't post the thread before `open-video.ai/try` and `discord.gg/open-video` resolve (the gate).
- Don't edit Tweet 1 after posting — editing kills the engagement score on X. If there's a typo,
  delete-and-repost within the first ~2 minutes, otherwise live with it.
- Don't tag `@Runway`/`@OpenAI`/`@GoogleDeepMind` in the main thread — it reads as clout-chasing
  and invites flagging. Save named comparisons for the sub-threads and replies where someone asks.
- Don't re-post the identical thread to a second account or re-thread it the same day; X suppresses
  duplicate content. If you want a second swing, do it 48h later with a different angle
  (e.g. "the quality loop, explained" with a clip).
