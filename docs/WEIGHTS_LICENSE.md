repository's code license.

The ComfyUI package/model card identifies the weights as being under the
**MiniMax H3 Community License**:

- [Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3 model card](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3)
- [Upstream MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3 repository](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3)
- [Upstream license text](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/raw/main/LICENSE)

The upstream license text fetched on 2026-08-07 is headed:

> “MINIMAX H3 COMMUNITY LICENSE AGREEMENT”

It defines the applicable territory as “worldwide, excluding the Excluded
Territories” and names the European Union, United Kingdom, Republic of Korea,
and United States of America as excluded territories. It also includes
commercial terms and use restrictions. For example, the fetched text says:

> “You may not use, reproduce, modify, distribute, or display the MiniMax H3

> “You shall obtain a separate, prior written authorization from MiniMax ... if
> your commercial products and services generate more than 20 million US
> dollars (or equivalent in other currencies) in yearly revenue.”

These quotations are provided to identify the license and demonstrate that
restrictions exist; they are not a substitute for the complete upstream
agreement. Users must review the current license themselves, including its
territorial, commercial, acceptable-use, and downstream-distribution terms,
before downloading or using the weights.
